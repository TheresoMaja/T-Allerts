Community SOS Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Effective date: 21 November 2026

1. About Community SOS

Community SOS is a community-based safety application designed to help people in South Africa request assistance from nearby registered members.

For privacy enquiries, contact us at theresomaja91bokamosoete2@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and permissions you grant, we may process:

  • Account information, including your email address and account identifier.
  • Location information when you use location-sharing or SOS features.
  • Emergency alert information, including alert details, timestamps and associated location.
  • Device and notification tokens needed to deliver notifications.
  • Membership status and payment transaction references.
  • Technical and diagnostic information needed to maintain the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To deliver SOS alerts to nearby opted-in members.
  • To share relevant emergency location information with intended alert recipients.
  • To send app and emergency notifications.
  • To manage membership payments through PayFast.
  • To protect the app and prevent misuse.

4. Location Information

Location information is used to identify nearby opted-in members and help them locate a person requesting assistance. When you send an SOS alert, the location associated with that alert may be shared with the intended recipients.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may affect location-based SOS features.

5. Sharing Information

Community SOS uses service providers such as Google Firebase to support authentication, data storage, cloud functions and notifications. PayFast processes membership payments.

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only as necessary to operate the app, deliver SOS alerts, process payments, protect users or comply with applicable law.

6. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for app operation, emergency functionality, payment records, security and legal obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.

Visit our Privacy Rights page for instructions.

8. Children's Privacy

Community SOS is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information without the required consent or authorisation.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
