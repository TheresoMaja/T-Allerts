Your Community SOS Privacy Rights

Your personal information and your rights.

Effective date: 21 November 2026

1. Your Privacy Matters

Community SOS respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access personal information associated with your account.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information.
  • Object to certain processing of your personal information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is collected and used.

3. How to Submit a Request

Send an email to:

theresomaja91bokamosoete2@gmail.com

Use the subject line: Community SOS Privacy Request.

Please include:

  • The email address linked to your Community SOS account.
  • The type of request you are making.
  • A clear explanation of what you would like us to do.

We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. Do not send passwords or full payment card details by email.

4. Account and Data Deletion

You may request account deletion by contacting us using the email address above. Deleting an account may affect your access to membership and SOS features.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for payment records, security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

5. Location Permissions

You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may prevent some location-based SOS features from working correctly.

6. Response to Requests

We will review privacy requests and respond within the timeframes required by applicable law. We may contact you if additional information is needed to verify your identity or understand your request.
